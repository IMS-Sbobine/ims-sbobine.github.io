Privacy

Last updated 15 October 2026

This is a private, student-run site where classmates share notes and study resources with each other. Access is protected by a class password.

What we collect

  • When you sign in (with Google or an emailed code): your name and email address. We don't get your Google password, contacts, files or anything else from your account.
  • What you do on the site: uploads, edits, access requests and sign-ups, and when you last signed in. These are logged with your email address so misuse can be traced.
  • Files you upload are stored in the site's Google Drive.
  • Usage counts: which pages are opened and which features are used (for example ticking a lecture or opening a suggested resource), so we can see whether the site helps. You appear in these as a scrambled code that can't be turned back into your email address; if you haven't signed in, as a random code your browser keeps. Nothing you type (searches, notes, topics) is recorded. The detailed counts are kept for two weeks, then only weekly totals, and the codes are deleted at the end of the semester.

We don't use cookies, ads or third-party trackers. Your device keeps a sign-in token so you stay signed in, and the random code mentioned above.

Who can see it

  • Admins (the classmates who run the site) can see the member list, access requests and the activity log.
  • Contributor names are shown only to approved members.
  • Some study resources are shared only with approved members; others can be opened by anyone with the link.

Your data isn't sold or shared with anyone else. It's stored with Google (Sheets, Drive and Gmail), the services the site runs on.

To suggest relevant resources for each lecture, the text of files on the class Drive and the lecture topics are processed by Anthropic and Voyage AI. Those files can include the names of the classmates who wrote them. Neither service gets the member list, email addresses or anything from your account, and neither uses what the site sends to train its models.

How long we keep it

Member details and logs are kept while the site is in use by the class, then deleted.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your details at any time by emailing ims-study-tracker-help@googlegroups.com. Leaving the email list only stops the emails.
